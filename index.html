78 in 2016. 57 in the last tub.

Every Christmas I tip a tub of Miniature Heroes onto the carpet, sort them into rows and count them. Now and then I do one mid-year too. Purely for science.

It's not scientific, to be fair. But ten years in, the numbers are pretty hard to ignore.

Twenty-one fewer heroes in the last tub than in 2016. Purple bars are Christmas tubs. Lilac ones are the mid-year extras, which is where the weirdest tub turned up.

By Christmas 2023 the tub had dropped from 600g to 550g, yet each chocolate got heavier: 8.6g in 2018, 9.6g now. Fewer heroes, slightly chunkier ones.

A typical tub, laid out

Same rows as on the carpet. One wrapper is one chocolate, averaged over the 15 tubs since the line-up grew from seven to nine and rounded.

The newcomers win. The Twirl is looking a bit lonely.
